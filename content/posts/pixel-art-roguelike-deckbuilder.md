## What is a roguelike deckbuilder?

A roguelike deckbuilder is a game where you build a deck of cards during a run, fight with it, and start over from scratch when you lose. Each run has random rewards, so the deck is different every time. Dream Quest (2014) is often cited as an early example, and Slay the Spire made the genre mainstream.

The three ideas that define it:

- **Deckbuilding during the run.** You start with a small, weak deck and add, remove or upgrade cards as you go.
- **Permadeath.** Losing ends the run. Some games keep small unlocks between runs, but the deck resets.
- **Randomness with choices.** Card rewards, shops and events are random, but you decide what to take.

Many games stretch the definition. Some use dice, tiles or slot machine symbols instead of cards, but the loop of building, testing and restarting is the same. That is why a few entries below are not strictly card games.

## Which ones are best for combo and score building?

Balatro and Luck be a Landlord are the best picks if you like breaking a scoring system. Both are about stacking effects until the numbers explode, with almost no traditional combat. Balatro uses poker hands and jokers; Luck be a Landlord uses slot machine symbols to pay rising rent.

**Balatro** is the obvious starting point. You play poker hands to beat score targets, and jokers change the rules: some multiply, some trigger on specific cards, some grow over the run. Its Steam reviews were Overwhelmingly Positive when we checked.

**Luck be a Landlord** is smaller and faster. Each spin of your slot machine pays coins based on which symbols land next to each other, and you add new symbols between spins. Synergies between symbols decide whether you make rent.

**Peglin** fits here too. You fire orbs into a pachinko board, and the pegs you hit decide the damage. Building a bag of orbs with good effects feels a lot like building a deck.

## Which ones focus on tactical combat?

Shogun Showdown, Cobalt Core and Dungeon Drafters are the most tactical picks. All three add positioning to the card play: where you stand, or where your ship is, matters as much as which card you use. They suit players who liked reading enemy intents in Slay the Spire.

**Shogun Showdown** puts you and your enemies on a single line of tiles. You queue attack tiles, turn around, move and time your hits, and every turn is a small puzzle. It is one of the most focused games on this list.

**Cobalt Core** is a space battle deckbuilder where your cards move your ship left and right to dodge shots and line up your weapons. It also has a time-loop story with a cast of crew members, which gives runs more personality than most.

**Dungeon Drafters** mixes card drafting with a grid-based dungeon crawler. Cards are spells and attacks you place on the grid, so it plays like a light tactics game.

## Which ones are easiest to start with?

Luck be a Landlord, Peglin and Slice & Dice are the easiest to start with. Their rules fit on one screen, runs are short, and you can learn by playing instead of reading. Card Quest and Shogun Showdown have a steeper first hour but reward patience.

**Slice & Dice** gives you a party of five heroes, each with a die. You roll, reroll and assign faces to attack, block or heal. Between fights, you level heroes and change their dice. The rules are simple, but the decisions get deep.

**Card Quest** is older (2017) and more traditional: you pick a class, fight through dungeons and build a deck around stamina and combos. It is a good choice if you like classic dungeon-crawl structure.

A practical tip for any of them: play your first few runs on the lowest difficulty and do not worry about losing. Every lost run teaches you which cards or symbols carry the deck.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Balatro a pixel art game?

Yes. Balatro uses a pixel art style with a CRT-like screen effect, and it carries the Pixel Graphics tag on Steam. Its Steam reviews were Overwhelmingly Positive on 17 November 2026.

### What was the first roguelike deckbuilder?

Dream Quest, released in 2014, is often cited as an early roguelike deckbuilder and an influence on Slay the Spire. Slay the Spire, released in full in 2019, is the game that made the genre popular.

### Are dice games like Slice & Dice roguelike deckbuilders?

Not strictly, since they use dice instead of cards. They share the same loop, though: building a set of options during a run, testing it in fights and starting over after a loss. Many players treat them as part of the same family.

### Which pixel art deckbuilder is best for short sessions?

Luck be a Landlord and Peglin have some of the shortest runs on this list, and both are easy to pause between spins or shots. Balatro also works well in short sessions.
