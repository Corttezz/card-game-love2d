# Games like Balatro and Slay the Spire: 12 roguelike deckbuilders to play next

*Updated on 2026-11-13*

Balatro and Slay the Spire scratch two different itches. **Balatro** (2024) is about breaking a scoring system: you stack jokers and multipliers until the numbers explode. **Slay the Spire** (2019) is about tactical card fights and route planning: you read enemy intents, pick a path on the map and shape a deck over a run.

Most lists mix the two without saying which one each game resembles. This one sorts them, so you can go straight to what you liked. Release dates come from each game's Steam store page, checked on 13 November 2026 ([Balatro](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2379780/), [Slay the Spire](https://store.steampowered.com/app/646570/)). None of the 12 picks is in Early Access.

## What are the best games like Balatro?

The closest games to Balatro are Luck be a Landlord (2023), Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers (2024), CloverPit (2025) and Peglin (full release 2024). All four are about building an engine that multiplies its own score, usually on a twist of a real game: slot machines, blackjack or pachinko. Runs are short and built for "one more try".

### 1. Luck be a Landlord (2023)
A slot machine deckbuilder: you add symbols to a reel instead of cards, and they trigger each other, with rent due every few spins. The closest feeling to Balatro's "one more synergy" loop. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1404850/), released 6 January 2023.

### 2. Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers (2024)
Blackjack turned into a roguelike deckbuilder. You collect odd cards that bend the rules of 21 and fight opponents hand by hand. Good if you liked Balatro's twist on a real card game. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2400510/), released 8 August 2024.

### 3. CloverPit (2025)
A slot-machine roguelite with a darker tone: its store page calls it a "slot machine nightmare" and a debt simulator. You buy charms and bend the odds to pay what you owe. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3314790/), released 26 September 2025.

### 4. Peglin (2024)
Pachinko meets Slay the Spire. You shoot orbs into a peg board, and the pegs you hit decide the damage. A good bridge between both games: map routing like Spire, chain reactions like Balatro. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1296610/), full release 27 August 2024.

## What games are like Slay the Spire?

The games closest to Slay the Spire are Monster Train (2020), Vault of the Void (2022), Cobalt Core (2023) and Wildfrost (2023). They keep its core: a hand of cards, a resource to spend each turn, readable enemy actions and a run that ends when you lose. Each one adds a single twist to that formula.

### 5. Monster Train (2020)
Card battles across three vertical floors of a train, defending a pyre at the top. Pairs of clans create big synergy builds. One of the closest matches to Spire in depth. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1102190/), released 21 May 2020; Monster Train 2 followed on 21 May 2025 ([Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2742830/)).

### 6. Vault of the Void (2022)
A deckbuilder that gives you more control over your deck and lets you swap cards often. Fewer bad draws, more planning. A strong pick if randomness in Spire frustrated you. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1135810/), released 4 October 2022.

### 7. Cobalt Core (2023)
Spaceship battles where your cards move the ship left and right to dodge and line up shots, tactics on a single axis. Crew members add their own cards. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2179850/), released 8 November 2023.

### 8. Wildfrost (2023)
A cute, tactical deckbuilder where every unit and enemy attacks on a countdown timer. Harder than it looks. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1811990/), released 12 April 2023.

## What deckbuilders have a great story or atmosphere?

If what kept you playing was the mood, try Inscryption (2021), Black Book (2021), Death Howl (2025) or Card Quest (2017). They put card fights inside a darker world, a story campaign or a map you explore. Most are less about endless runs and more about one journey you remember.

### 9. Inscryption (2021)
Starts as a dark card game in a cabin and turns into something else entirely. Less replayable than the others, but very hard to forget. Go in knowing as little as possible. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1092790/), released 19 October 2021.

### 10. Black Book (2021)
A dark RPG built on Slavic myths, with card battles inside a story campaign. For players who want Spire-style fights with a narrative around them. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1138660/), released 10 August 2021.

### 11. Death Howl (2025)
An open-world deckbuilder with grid-based combat and more than 160 cards, according to its store page. Tactical positioning matters as much as the cards you draw. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2825880/), released 9 December 2025.

### 12. Card Quest (2017)
Dungeon crawling with card combat, where the class you pick decides your deck. Short runs and a lot of build variety for its size. [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/493080/), released 7 November 2017.

## What makes a good roguelike deckbuilder?

Under the different themes, the best roguelike deckbuilders share three design ideas: your deck is your character, removing cards is as strong as adding them, and every run makes you commit to a few choices and live with them. If those ideas are what you love, look past the theme to the mechanics.

1. **Your deck is your character.** You don't level up a hero. You change what is in your deck, and that changes how you play.
2. **Removing is as strong as adding.** The best decks are often thin. Every Spire veteran knows the value of removing a Strike.
3. **Each run asks you to commit.** You see a few options, pick one, and live with it. That is what makes "one more run" so strong.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is the closest game to Slay the Spire?
Monster Train is the one most often named, for its card battles, synergies and run structure. Vault of the Void and Cobalt Core are also close, each with a twist.

### Is there a game like Balatro?
Luck be a Landlord is the closest in feel: you build a slot machine engine that multiplies its own score. Dungeons & Degenerate Gamblers, CloverPit and Peglin are other good picks.

### Are Balatro and Slay the Spire the same genre?
Both are roguelike deckbuilders, but they play differently. Balatro is about scoring combos on poker hands; Slay the Spire is about tactical fights against enemies on a branching map.

### Is Slay the Spire 2 out?
It is in Early Access. It launched on Steam on 5 March 2026 and is still receiving updates before its full release.

### What are the best roguelike deckbuilders for beginners?
Peglin, Wildfrost and Luck be a Landlord have simple rules and short runs. Slay the Spire itself is also very beginner-friendly at the lowest difficulty.
