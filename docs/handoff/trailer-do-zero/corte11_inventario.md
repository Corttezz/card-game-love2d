## e) Why quality dropped in cut 10d (measured)

| file | resolution | video bitrate | sharpness (Laplacian variance, hall frame, same crop) |
|---|---|---|---|
| v8 master | 1920x1080 60 | **28,9 Mb/s** (crf 14 slow) | n/a |
| v6 master | 1920x1080 60 | 28,2 Mb/s | n/a |
| 10d 1080p | 1920x1080 60 | 13,4 Mb/s | 281 / 340 |
| **10d 720p (the file Daniel got)** | 1280x720 60 | **5,7 Mb/s** | **60 / 67** |
| Higgsfield 4K source | 3840x2160 30 | 22 Mb/s | 327 / 336 |

Causes:
1. **The 720p delivery.** Pixel art with scanlines scaled by 1,5 (not an integer) at 5,7 Mb/s loses about 80% of the detail (sharpness 281 → 60). Every earlier cut was delivered as a 1080p master at ~29 Mb/s.
2. **The AI halls were "treated"** in trailer-do-zero (luminance percentiles, emissive layer, chroma by hue, palette of the real hall). v6/v8 used the original with a lanczos scale, which is the rule written after v3 ("do not re-pixelize: the owner saw the quality drop").
3. **The CRT applied offline** over takes captured without CRT, in 600-frame chunks, after a re-encode chain (take yuv444 crf 8 → clip → montage → CRT chunk → concat → mux → 720p). The v8 takes carry the game's own CRT and bezel from the recorder, with one encode at the end.

Fix for cut 11: v8/v6 takes with the in-game CRT, halls straight from `ai_4k` (lanczos, no palette), the v8 engine (`trailer_v3.py`, one encode at crf 14 slow), and the 720p made from that master with lanczos at high quality (crf 14, measured below), plus the 1080p master.
